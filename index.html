Privacy Policy

At SPLIT, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by SPLIT and how we use it.

Client-Side Processing

To ensure your maximum privacy and security, all file processing (such as Image to PDF and Video to MP3 conversions) happens entirely inside your browser (Client-side). Your files are never uploaded to any of our servers. Once you close the tab, the data is gone forever.

Log Files & Cookies

SPLIT follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. We also use third-party advertisements (like Google AdSense) to support our site. These advertisers may use cookies to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.
